About Us

A Local Business
Built on Trust

Hello, I'm Terry Thoms. Myself, my wonderful wife, and our fantastic sons are the people working our business. We've lived in Chilliwack for over 25 years — we've watched this town grow into a city, and we have a vested interest in keeping it the great place to live that it is.

One way we can do that is to keep the lawns and gardens of the people in this city looking great. We already service properties throughout the area and would love to take care of yours too.

We work with homeowners, landlords, commercial properties, and strata. We always show up on time, do the job right, and leave your property looking better than when we arrived.

We're not a big franchise. We're your neighbours, and your yard matters to us.
