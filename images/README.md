## Tips

- JPEGs work best for photos; PNGs work best for graphics/illustrations.
- Aim for images under 300 KB each (compress at [tinypng.com](https://tinypng.com) or [squoosh.app](https://squoosh.app)).
- `hero-bg`: 1920×1080 or larger; gallery/about images: at least 800 px wide.
